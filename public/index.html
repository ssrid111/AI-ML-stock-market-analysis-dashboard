UNDER THE HOOD

A starting point for your ML project

This dashboard retrieves daily price history from Yahoo Finance through its server. The experimental model fits a linear regression to the last 30 trading observations. It does not use an external AI service.

How the forecast works

The model fits a straight line to recent prices and projects that trend forward. The displayed range uses historical residual variation and widens with the horizon. It is illustrative, not a calibrated confidence interval.

Build toward real research

Connect historical data, validate chronological train/test splits, compare against a persistence baseline, and evaluate MAE and RMSE before presenting a trained model.
